BraveBot

What a BraveBot session may ask this browser for. BraveBot also asks you before each call.

Presets

A preset turns on the groups it names and turns off every other group. It asks Brave for everything those groups need in one prompt, after you confirm. The groups it turns on stay on until you turn them off.

Groups

Each group is off until you turn it on here and Brave grants what it needs, for as long as you choose: until you turn it off, until Brave restarts, or one hour. Turning a group off takes back what no other group still on uses, and a permission you take back in Brave's extension settings turns its group off. Open a group to switch single tools.

Allowed sites

A group that reads pages reaches only the sites written here, unless you choose all sites for it above. Nothing is allowed at first, so turning such a group on reaches no page until a site is added. Saving asks Brave for access to the sites, which it accepts only from this click, and gives back the access to a site you take off the list. A site that is also under Blocked sites is blocked. Write one site to a line, such as https://news.example.com. A site is matched as written: https://example.com does not cover https://www.example.com or http://example.com. Brave's own site access setting for the extension can still withhold a site that is listed here.

Blocked sites

No tool lists, searches, reads or opens a site written here, whatever is switched on above, and a call that names one is refused. Tabs, frames, history and bookmarks at a blocked site are left out of results, so a list can hold fewer rows than Brave has. Write one site to a line, such as https://mail.example.com. A site is matched as written: https://example.com does not cover https://www.example.com or http://example.com.

Activity

The last 200 calls that reached this extension, newest first, whichever session made them and whether or not you are watching it. Each shows when it arrived, the group and tool it named, and whether it ran, was refused or failed. A call that names a page shows the site of its address, and nothing else of it. No page text, search text or title is kept. A call to a group that is off is listed too, as refused.
