What a BraveBot session may ask this browser for. BraveBot also asks you before each call.

Blocked sites

No tool lists, searches, reads or opens a site written here, whatever is switched on above, and a call that names one is refused. Tabs, frames, history and bookmarks at a blocked site are left out of results, so a list can hold fewer rows than Brave has. Write one site to a line, such as https://mail.example.com. A site is matched as written: https://example.com does not cover https://www.example.com or http://example.com.
