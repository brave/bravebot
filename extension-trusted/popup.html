BraveBot Trusted Input

This extension sends real mouse clicks, typing and key presses to a page, takes a picture of all of a page, reads what a page wrote to its console, and chooses a file of yours on a page's file input, when the BraveBot extension asks and you allow each action in a window of its own. It takes requests from the BraveBot extension () and from nothing else, and it does nothing on its own.

While it is turned on, Brave lets it use the debugger on every site, which is why Brave lists it as reading and changing your data on all websites. Turn it off whenever you do not want BraveBot to use real input, take a picture of a whole page, read a console or give a file to a page.

To turn it off, open brave://extensions and turn BraveBot Trusted Input off. To remove it, choose Remove there.
